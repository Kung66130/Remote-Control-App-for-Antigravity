This policy describes the Android client Antigravity Remote. Before publication, the developer must supply a public contact address and confirm the service-related disclosures below.

## Connection and remote content

You choose a remote URL or scan a QR code to connect. The app stores the most recently used URL in its private local preferences for reconnection. A remote URL may contain access credentials; protect it as you would a password. The embedded browser may store cookies, session information and cached page content on your device.

When you use the remote page, information you enter, including prompts, messages, code and selected attachments, is sent to the remote service you connected to. That service and its hosting or AI providers process information under their own policies. The Android app does not make all remote-service processing local or anonymous. HTTP or local-network connections may not encrypt traffic; use trusted HTTPS remote links.

## Permissions and device features

- Camera: used when you choose to scan a connection QR code. The scanner processes frames on the device. The client does not intentionally upload camera frames for this scanning function.
- Files and photos: the system chooser lets you select particular files. The app does not request broad access to your photo library. Files you attach can be uploaded to the connected remote service.
- Clipboard: the app reads clipboard text when you use its paste-and-connect action.
- Text-to-speech: text selected for speech is passed to the configured Android speech engine. Whether that engine uses an online service depends on the engine and device settings.
- Network: used to load and interact with the remote service. Connection providers can receive information such as your IP address and browser headers.

## Storage and deletion

Stored connection information and browser data remain on the device until cleared. To remove the client's local data, open Android Settings, select Antigravity Remote, and clear its storage, or uninstall it. Clearing local data does not delete information held by your remote host or third-party services; use those services' controls or contact their providers. This release disables app-data backup and excludes app data from cloud backup and device transfer rules.

## Advertising and analytics

No advertising or dedicated analytics SDK was found in the Android client dependencies reviewed for this release. Embedded remote pages and third-party libraries or services may have their own processing; their behavior must be checked before submitting Data safety answers. Do not interpret this statement as a claim that no data ever leaves the device.
